Do your short track blade ends have to be rounded? Yes, and checking takes two minutes.

The ISU rule is exact: tubes closed and blade ends rounded to a minimum radius of 10 mm. No points at either end of either blade.

To check, look at each tip from the side and hold it against a 20 mm circle printed on paper. If the blade curves tighter than the circle, that end needs shaping. Do all four ends, front and back on both blades, with cut resistant gloves on.

If a tip needs work, clamp it in a jig and follow the curve with a bench stone. Shape the ends only. Your rocker, bend and edges live on the running surface.
