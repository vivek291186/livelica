Short track blade ends must be rounded to a minimum radius of 10 mm, with the tubes closed. That is the ISU rule, and you can check your own blades in two minutes at the kitchen table.

Hold each tip against a 20 mm circle printed on paper. If the steel curves tighter than the circle, the end needs shaping. Check front and back on both blades, especially after a crash, after travel and at the start of a season.
