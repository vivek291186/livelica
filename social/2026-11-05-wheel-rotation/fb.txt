Flip every wheel when you rotate. That one habit does most of the work of stopping cone wear, the uneven slope that makes inline speed wheels lose grip and wear out early.

Our wheel care guide covers how often to rotate (every one to three sessions for most skaters), a simple pattern for four wheel, three wheel and Hi Lo frames, a quick bearing check while the wheels are off, and how to tell when a set is actually finished.
