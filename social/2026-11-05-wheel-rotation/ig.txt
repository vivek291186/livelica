Most race wheels wear out long before they need to.

The urethane is rarely the problem. Uneven wear is. You push to the outside, lean into corners and favour one side, so the inner edge of each wheel grinds lower until it turns into a cone with less grip.

The fix takes about ten minutes. Every one to three sessions, take the wheels off, flip every wheel so the worn edge faces out, and move the most worn wheels into the positions that see the least load. Run a fingertip across each wheel after skating. If you feel a slope, it is time.

Running three wheels or a Hi Lo frame? The swipe covers both.
