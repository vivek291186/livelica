New to inline speed skating and lost in the jargon? Start here.

Your platform is the boot plus the frame. The boot holds your foot and ankle, and the frame is the stiff beam that keeps your wheels in a straight line so your push goes into the road.

Most beginners are well served on 90mm to 100mm wheels. Durometer is wheel hardness: XFirm is softer with more grip, XXFirm is harder and rolls faster on smooth surfaces.

Check the bore before you order bearings. Inline speed uses a 608 with an 8mm bore, while many quads use a 627 with a 7mm bore that will not fit. And keep them clean, because neglect is what kills bearings, not age.
