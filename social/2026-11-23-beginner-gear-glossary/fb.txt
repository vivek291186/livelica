Buying a first speed setup? Fit the boot first. A boot that fits your foot, locks your heel down and does not crush your toes will teach you more than any wheel.

Then a four wheel frame on 90mm or 100mm wheels keeps a new skater low and stable. Pick a wheel firmness for your surface, not the hardest one available. Finish with a solid steel bearing set, correct spacers and thin skate oil, and change one thing at a time when you upgrade.
